Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

View All FAQs
How much do your services cost?+

Product Finding is KSh 100, Order Assistance is KSh 150, and Multiple Product Search starts at KSh 200. Business requests are quoted individually.

Do you handle the payment?+

No. You pay the marketplace directly through your own account for the product and delivery. You pay our service fee to us separately.

Are you affiliated with Jumia?+

No. Ordexa is an independent shopping assistance service. We help you use marketplaces like Jumia more easily — we're not operated by or affiliated with them.

Can you help me find a specific product?+

Yes — tell us what you need, your budget, and any preferences, and we'll find options that match.

What if I need help after I place my order?+

We'll help you track your order and follow up on delivery. Product quality, delivery and refunds remain the seller's and marketplace's responsibility.

Do you support businesses too?+

Yes — we help small businesses find and order supplies and equipment. These requests are quoted per job.
